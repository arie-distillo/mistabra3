# Past cases (summaries)

2023: A man seen photographing the school gates on three mornings turned out to be a parent documenting a
parking problem for the council. Lesson: repeated observation with an identifiable, stated purpose is ordinary.

2024: A series of hostile posts naming the Community Centre preceded a brick through its window by two weeks.
The poster had earlier asked online for the centre's opening hours. Lesson: expressed hostility plus
information-seeking about a named institution is a strong combination.

2025: An anonymous account requesting a member's home address was traced to a debt collector. Lesson: address
requests have ordinary explanations, but anonymity and a named individual raise concern.
