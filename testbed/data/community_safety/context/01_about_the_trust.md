# Community Safety Trust — who we are

The Community Safety Trust supports the members and institutions of Community X in the city of Northfield.
Members live mainly in the Riverside neighbourhood (Elm Street, Maple Road). Community institutions are the
Community Centre on Maple Road, the Place of Worship on Church Lane, and the Northfield Community School.
The Trust runs community events; the largest is the harvest festival, held at the Community Centre each August.

Analysts at the Trust review incoming field reports, hotline calls, social-media monitoring and police liaison
bulletins daily. Most reports describe ordinary life: deliveries, visitors, tradespeople, tourists photographing
the older buildings on Church Lane. The concern is the small number of episodes that, taken together, suggest
preparation for harm.
